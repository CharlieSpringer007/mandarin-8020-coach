# Mandarin 80/20 Coach

A free web app for learning to speak, read and write Mandarin Chinese. It works on Android, iPhone and any computer browser, with no sign-up.

**Open it:** https://charliespringer007.github.io/mandarin-8020-coach/

## What's inside
- **Course:** 6 modules and 36 classes, from pinyin and tones to real conversations, with action steps and progress ticks.
- **Goals & Tracker:** your why, HSK target, daily targets, a streak and a 7-day chart.
- **Lessons:** the core 1,000 high-frequency words, five steps each: character breakdown, Anki card, a graded story with pinyin/English toggles, a teacher's clinic, and the 5-sentence challenge with native-speaker style feedback.
- **Speak:** say a word or sentence and see which syllables and tones came through (microphone in Chrome and Edge, or your phone keyboard's microphone anywhere).
- **Deck & Anki:** export your cards for AnkiDroid, AnkiMobile or Anki desktop.

## Free vs. AI features
Everything above is free except the AI parts: custom lessons, sentence feedback and the chat coach. Those need your own API key, added on the Profile tab. Either works:

- **Google Gemini (free to start):** get a key at [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com) › Get API key. The free tier has daily limits.
- **Anthropic Claude (pay as you go):** get a key at [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com). Roughly 10p per full lesson on Claude Sonnet 5.5, under 1p on Haiku 5.5.

Your key stays in your browser and is only sent to the provider it belongs to.

## Install on your phone
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, then ⋮ › **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, then Share › **Add to Home Screen**.

Progress is saved on your device. Use **Profile › Download backup** to move it to another device.
